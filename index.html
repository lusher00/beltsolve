notes & conventions
• Slack = belt path length − geometric path at the actual center. Positive = belt is long (tensionable); negative = belt is short (interference — size up or pull centers in).
• Exact C = the center distance where that pulley pair tensions the chosen belt with zero slack.
• Belt path length responds ~2× center distance (dL/dC ≈ 2 for d≪C), so 1 mm of center ≈ 2 mm of belt.
• Idler take-up shown is a rough midspan estimate; real take-up depends on idler position and wrap.
• Tooth range and pitch are yours to set. Pitch diameter, not OD, drives the geometry.
